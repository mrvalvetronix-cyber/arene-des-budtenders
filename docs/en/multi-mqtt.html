❓ How do I join a game?
  1. The host creates the room: they enter their nickname, tap “Create a game”, pick the mode and teams, then share the 4-character code shown at the top of the lobby.
  2. To join: enter your nickname, tap “Join with a code”, type the 4-character code you got and tap “Join the game”.
  3. The host starts the round once everyone’s in. Good luck! 🌿
